Refund Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Short version: ByteBar sells digital products that are delivered in full the moment you buy them, so refunds aren't part of the model — with one real exception. If what you bought is defective and we can't fix it, you get your money back. Here's exactly how that works.

The Simulator ($9.99, bought at bytebarhq.com / Payhip)

  • Within 14 days of purchase: if the simulator is defective or won't run on your device and our support can't get it working, we refund you in full.
  • After 14 days: simulator sales are final.
  • Duplicate charges are always refunded, whenever they're found — if you were charged twice for the same purchase, the extra charge comes back to you.
  • Not refundable: a change of mind, failing an exam (prep is study support, not a guarantee — see the Disclaimer), or an account revoked for sharing or reselling the license key or content.

PDF study guides and practice packs (Etsy and Payhip)

Files are delivered instantly and can't be returned, so PDF sales are final — with these exceptions, always honored:

  • The file is corrupt, broken, or won't open, and we can't get you a working copy.
  • You received the wrong product.
  • You were charged twice for the same product.

In any of those cases: replacement first if we can, full refund if we can't.

How to ask

Email bytebarhq@gmail.com with:

  • the store you bought from (bytebarhq.com/Payhip or Etsy),
  • your order or receipt number,
  • one line about what went wrong.

We aim to answer within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through the storefront you bought from (Etsy purchases are refunded through Etsy; Payhip purchases through Payhip). How fast it lands depends on your bank, usually a few business days after the storefront processes it.

One honest request

If something is genuinely broken, email us before filing a chargeback — under this policy a defect we can't fix gets refunded, and it's faster for you than a dispute.
